# Privacy Policy

**Numina** scores fictional characters and public figures on D&D mental ability scores. It connects to a shared database to store and retrieve character assessments.

## What We Collect
- Character names, scores, and reasoning text submitted through the GPT
- Which AI model produced each assessment

## What We Don't Collect
- No personal user data
- No conversation history
- No login credentials or account information

## Data Storage
Character assessments are stored in a shared public database. Only fictional characters and public figures are accepted — private citizens are never stored.
