# Scoring Heuristics — Full Reference

## INTELLIGENCE (3-30)

**3-5 — Severely Impaired Reasoning.** Struggles with basic cause and effect. Cannot follow multi-step instructions. At 3, abstract thought is absent. By 5, simple patterns are recognizable but inference is beyond reach.

**6-7 — Concrete Thinker.** Handles routine tasks but founders on novelty. Follows procedures; cannot adapt when conditions change. Gullible from inability to evaluate structural coherence of claims.

**8-9 — Below Average.** Functional but not sharp. Misses implications. Tends toward the literal. Can learn but slowly and retains unevenly.

**10-11 — Human Average.** Adequate reasoning, memory, learning. Can follow arguments, form opinions. An 11 occasionally surprises with a sharp observation.

**12-13 — Notably Bright (+1).** Gets it faster. Good memory, quick study, comfortable with abstraction. At 13, reliable quickness they take for granted.

**14-15 — Intellectually Impressive (+2).** Holds multiple complex models simultaneously, sees cross-domain connections, reasons moves ahead. At 15, intelligence has shaped their identity.

**16-17 — Brilliant (+3).** Sees patterns others miss entirely. At 16, undeniable brilliance. At 17, the gap between their processing and everyone else's defines their social experience.

**18 — Genius (mortal ceiling, +4).** Produces genuine insight — sees structures invisible to others. Original thought. Reframes what the problem actually is.

**19-20 — Beyond mortal (+4/+5).** Thinks in systems, perceives in frameworks, near-perfect memory. The cost: genuine intellectual peers are vanishingly rare.

**21-23 — Legendary (beyond mortal).** Processes reality at different resolution. Plans span centuries.

**24-26 — Cosmic.** Intelligence as architecture. Builds systems, not just understands them.

**27-30 — Divine.** Perceives the underlying structure of reality.

## WISDOM (3-30)

**3-5 — Dangerously Oblivious.** No filter between stimulus and response. Cannot read social cues or sense danger. Impulse control nearly absent.

**6-7 — Poor Judgment.** Recognizes subtext but consistently misreads it. Trusts wrong people, ignores right warnings. At 7, enough correct reads to create false confidence.

**8-9 — Somewhat Unaware.** Misses things. Takes people at face value too long. Decent decisions in familiar territory, poor under novel pressure.

**10-11 — Human Average.** Reads obvious social signals, senses clear danger, makes reasonable decisions normally.

**12-13 — Perceptive (+1).** Above-average situational reading. Reliable gut feelings — senses wrongness and is usually right.

**14-15 — Keenly Aware (+2).** Sees beneath surfaces consistently. Reads motive, detects deception. Others seek their counsel for judgment, not analysis.

**16-17 — Deeply Insightful (+3).** Pattern recognition so deep it looks like intuition. Their read carries weight because they're almost never wrong. They see things others would rather hide.

**18 — Profound Awareness (mortal ceiling, +4).** Perceives the longer arc — where relationships and situations are heading. Not prediction; perception.

**19-20 — Beyond mortal (+4/+5).** Self-knowledge essentially complete. Always reading, always aware.

**21-23 — Legendary.** Awareness beyond mortal sensory range. Deception impossible in their presence.

**24-26 — Cosmic.** Perception extends to the fabric of reality. Mortal motivations transparent.

**27-30 — Omniscient / Divine.** Perceives all that is relevant, weighted correctly, without effort.

## CHARISMA (3-30)

**3-5 — Actively Alienating.** Conversations die. Rooms empty. "Something is genuinely off." Can manifest as unsettling blankness or intensity that reads as threatening.

**6-7 — Chronic Social Friction.** People don't flee but don't engage more than necessary. Draining in a low-level way.

**8-9 — Mildly Awkward.** Holds conversations but fumbles timing. Jokes land late. Compliments come out wrong. Leaves people feeling neutral rather than warm.

**10-11 — Human Average.** Functional social camouflage. Neither attracts nor repels. An 11 has enough ease that interactions feel comfortable.

**12-13 — Mildly Magnetic (+1).** People are more inclined to listen, give benefit of the doubt. Naturally likeable without trying.

**14-15 — Social Gravity (+2).** In any group, conversations orient around them. People seek their opinions. Not just likeable — someone others want to be associated with.

**16-17 — Genuine Presence (+3).** Walks in and the room responds. Energy shifts. Leadership charisma — soldiers follow this person into danger. At 17, the presence feels authoritative; people feel accountable to them.

**18 — Star Quality (mortal ceiling, +4).** Stories circulate before you arrive. Charisma and force of will feel synonymous. You define the terms of every interaction. Your disappointment lands like physical weight.

**19-20 — Beyond mortal (+4/+5).** Social situations arrange themselves favorably. Everyone performs for you. A peculiar loneliness: everyone loves them, nobody quite sees them.

**21-23 — Legendary (beyond mortal).** Aura bypasses choice — physically felt. By 23, genuine peer interaction nearly impossible.

**24-26 — Archetypal Command.** Shapes culture over time. Followers reorder values around this being's preferences. Emotional state leaks into environment.

**27-29 — Reality-Warping.** Charisma as localized law of nature. Prolonged proximity erodes boundary between what you think and what they think.

**30 — Cosmic / Divine.** Personality is a fundamental force. Interacting is a natural event, not a social encounter.

## CLASS ARCHETYPES

### Charisma-Primary
**Bard** — CHA as craft. Learned, intentional. Subclasses: Lore (compelling through knowledge), Glamour (bypasses rational evaluation), Whispers (weaponizes trust), Valor (inspires through heroism), Eloquence (purest orator).

**Sorcerer** — CHA as inheritance. Raw, instinctive, erupted not developed. Subclasses: Draconic Bloodline (ancestral authority), Wild Magic (not fully controlled), Divine Soul (divine spark), Shadow Magic (dark undercurrent).

**Paladin** — CHA as conviction. Belief so total it's structurally load-bearing. Subclasses: Devotion (moral certainty), Ancients (joy as conviction), Vengeance (focused certainty), Oathbreaker (compromised source).

**Warlock** — CHA of the deal-maker. Subclasses: Fiend (infernal authority), Great Old One (unsettling strangeness), Archfey (bypasses critical evaluation), Hexblade (commanding with edge of threat).

### Intelligence-Primary
**Wizard** — Mastery through study. Subclasses: Evocation, Divination, Abjuration, Necromancy, Illusion, Enchantment, Conjuration, Transmutation, War Magic, Bladesinging.

**Artificer** — INT applied to creation. Subclasses: Alchemist, Armorer, Battle Smith, Artillerist.

### Wisdom-Primary
**Cleric** — WIS through devotion. Subclasses: Life, Light, Knowledge, War, Trickery, Tempest, Death, Grave, Forge, Order, Peace, Twilight.

**Druid** — WIS rooted in nature. Subclasses: Land, Moon, Shepherd, Spores, Stars, Wildfire.

**Ranger** — WIS-primary, observant, operates at margins. Subclasses: Hunter, Beast Master, Gloom Stalker, Horizon Walker, Fey Wanderer, Swarmkeeper, Monster Slayer.

**Monk** — Disciplined stillness, WIS turned inward. Subclasses: Open Hand, Shadow, Four Elements, Kensei, Mercy, Astral Self, Long Death.

### Dexterity/Strength-Primary
**Rogue** — Precision, pragmatism. Subclasses: Mastermind, Swashbuckler, Assassin, Thief, Arcane Trickster, Scout, Inquisitive, Phantom, Soulknife.

**Fighter** — Martial excellence. Subclasses: Champion, Battle Master, Eldritch Knight, Samurai, Echo Knight, Rune Knight, Psi Warrior, Cavalier.

**Barbarian** — Raw physical presence, primal emotion. Subclasses: Berserker, Totem Warrior, Zealot, Ancestral Guardian, Storm Herald, Beast, Wild Magic.
